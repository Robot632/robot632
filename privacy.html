Who runs this site

This is a personal hobby website by Robot632. It is not a business, it sells nothing and it has no accounts, forms, ads or newsletters. You can contact me through GitHub or YouTube.

Privacy policy

I do not collect, store or sell any personal data. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no cookies on this site. Nothing you type here (including in the terminal page) is sent anywhere — it all runs in your browser.

The site saves a few small settings only on your own device, using your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and are used only to make the site work the way you left it:

  • robot632_theme – light or dark theme
  • robot632_sound – sound on or off
  • robot632_tagline_index – which tagline to show next
  • robot632_visits – the “powered on X times” counter (counted locally, not by me)
  • robot632_graphics – Low or High graphics
  • robot632_pet – how full Pip the pet robot is, and when you last checked on it
  • robot632_best_flappy, robot632_best_snake, robot632_best_reflex – your best scores on the games page
  • robot632_games_tab – which game you played last

Because these are strictly functional and never sent to me or to anyone else, no cookie banner is needed.
